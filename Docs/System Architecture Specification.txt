# Tnvios System Architecture Specification (SAS)

Version: 1.0

Status: Master Architecture Document

Classification: Core Platform Blueprint

---

# Executive Summary

Tnvios is a multi-tenant, modular, enterprise-grade Business Operating System designed to support:

* Group Headquarters
* Business Units
* Departments
* Employees
* Customers
* Vendors
* Partners
* AI Agents

Tnvios follows a Platform-First architecture where all business capabilities are implemented as installable modules on top of shared platform engines.

---

# Architectural Principles

## Platform First

Core platform provides reusable services.

Modules consume platform services.

Modules do not implement platform infrastructure.

---

## Modular Architecture

Every business capability is a module.

Examples:

CRM

HR

Payroll

Finance

Projects

Travel

Healthcare

Manufacturing

Training

Events

Research

---

## Event Driven

Modules communicate through events.

No direct module-to-module dependencies.

---

## AI Native

AI is a platform capability.

Not a separate application.

---

## Enterprise Security

Every request is:

Authenticated

Authorized

Audited

Tenant Isolated

Encrypted

---

## Cloud Native

Designed for:

Docker

Kubernetes

Horizontal Scaling

Multi Region Deployment
