# Tnvios Workflow Engine Specification

Version: 1.0

Status: Core Platform Specification

Classification: Business Process Engine

Priority: Critical

---

# Executive Summary

The Workflow Engine provides a centralized platform for defining, executing, monitoring, automating, and auditing business processes across Tnvios.

The Workflow Engine is used by:

* Core Platform
* CRM
* Finance
* HR
* Payroll
* Procurement
* Projects
* Customer Portal
* Vendor Portal
* Partner Portal
* AI Agents

All business approvals and process automation must flow through the Workflow Engine.

---

# Purpose

The Workflow Engine manages:

* Business Processes
* Approvals
* Tasks
* Routing
* Escalations
* Notifications
* AI Actions
* Integrations
* Automation Rules

# Golden Rule

Every business process in Tnvios should be represented as a workflow.

Instead of coding:

```text id="40v2kk"
if manager approves
 then finance approves
```

Tnvios should execute:

```text id="b73s0x"
Workflow Definition
 ↓
Workflow Instance
 ↓
Workflow Engine
 ↓
Outcome
```

This makes approvals, automation, compliance, AI participation, and enterprise customization possible without changing application code.

# Enterprise Workflow Integrations

The Workflow Engine integrates with onboarding, comments, inbound email, and import/export.

Supported workflow triggers:

```text
onboarding.step.completed
comment.created
comment.mention.created
inbound_email.received
inbound_email.action.requested
import.completed
export.requested
```

Email-to-action may approve, reject, comment, request changes, or create tasks only after permission and policy validation. Sensitive finance, payroll, procurement, HR, and security actions may require additional confirmation or MFA.
