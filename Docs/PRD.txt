# Tnvios Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Version: 1.0

Status: Vision & Foundation

Author: Founder

---

# Executive Summary

Tnvios is a modular enterprise operating system that enables organizations of all sizes to manage their entire business ecosystem through a single platform.

Unlike traditional ERP, CRM, or HR systems, Tnvios is built around organizational hierarchy and business relationships.

Tnvios serves:

* Group Headquarters
* Business Units
* Departments
* Employees
* Customers
* Vendors
* Partners
* AI Agents

All users operate within a unified platform, identity system, workflow engine, analytics engine, and module ecosystem.

The platform provides a core operating layer and installable business modules.

---

# Vision

To become the operating system for organizations worldwide.

Tnvios should allow any organization to operate, manage, analyze, automate, and scale all business activities through a single platform.
