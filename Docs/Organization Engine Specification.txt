# Tnvios Organization Engine Specification

Version: 1.0

Status: Core Platform Specification

Classification: Foundational Engine

Priority: Critical

---

# Executive Summary

The Organization Engine is the central engine of Tnvios.

Every user, module, permission, workflow, dashboard, report, AI agent, customer, vendor, and partner operates within the organizational hierarchy managed by this engine.

The Organization Engine serves as the system of record for organizational structure.

All platform services depend on it.

---

# Purpose

The Organization Engine provides:

* Multi-Tenant Organization Management
* Corporate Hierarchy Management
* Business Unit Management
* Department Management
* Team Management
* User Membership Management
* Reporting Relationships
* Shared Services Structures
* Cross-Organization Relationships

---

# Design Principles

## Single Source of Truth

Organization structure exists only within the Organization Engine.

Other modules consume organization data.

Modules must not maintain their own hierarchy structures.

---

## Hierarchy Driven

Every entity exists within an organizational context.

---

## Enterprise Ready

Must support:

* Startups
* SMEs
* Enterprises
* Conglomerates
* Government Organizations

---

## Flexible

Support:

* Traditional hierarchy
* Matrix organizations
* Holding companies
* Shared service centers

# Organizational Model

## Level 0: Tenant

Highest logical boundary.

Represents:

* Customer account
* Company account
* Government account

Example:

```text
Tenant
 └── Tata Group
```

Responsibilities:

* Subscription
* Billing
* Global Settings
* Security Policies

---

# Level 1: Group Headquarters

Represents:

Parent organization.

Examples:

```text
Tata Group

Aditya Birla Group

Reliance Industries
```

Responsibilities:

* Governance
* Strategy
* Treasury
* Risk
* Group Analytics

---

# Level 2: Organization

Represents legal entities.

Example:

```text
Tata Group

 ├── Tata Motors
 ├── Tata Steel
 ├── Tata Consultancy Services
 └── Taj Hotels
```

Each organization has:

* Legal identity
* Tax identity
* Compliance requirements

---

# Level 3: Business Unit

Represents operational divisions.

Example:

```text
Tata Motors

 ├── Passenger Vehicles
 ├── Commercial Vehicles
 ├── EV Division
 └── International Operations
```

Responsibilities:

* Revenue ownership
* Cost ownership
* Operational KPIs

---

# Level 4: Department

Represents functional areas.

Examples:

```text
Finance

HR

Sales

Marketing

Operations

IT

Legal
```

---

# Level 5: Team

Represents working groups.

Examples:

```text
Recruitment Team

Payroll Team

Digital Marketing Team

Enterprise Sales Team
```

---

# Level 6: User

Represents platform members.

Types:

Employee

Contractor

Consultant

Intern

Executive

# Architecture Rule

No module may create or manage its own organizational hierarchy.

All organizational structures must be created, managed, and resolved through the Organization Engine.

The Organization Engine is the authoritative source for all organizational relationships within Tnvios.
